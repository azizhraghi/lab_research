## Overall Inspiration

Combine the user experience of:

* Medium (reading experience)
* Google Scholar (research discovery)
* Nature (scientific credibility)
* Perplexity AI (intelligent search)
* LinkedIn (researcher profiles)
* ArcGIS Dashboard (maps and data)
* Notion (clean organization)
* Vercel (modern aesthetics)
* Apple Human Interface Guidelines (simplicity)
* Material Design 3 (consistency)

The final interface should feel like a world-class scientific research ecosystem where visitors can effortlessly discover knowledge, connect with experts, and explore cutting-edge research through an AI-powered experience.
